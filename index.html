WHO IS BEHIND THIS

Alesia Yuzhakov, host and author. Sixteen years in small business, and the last two in AI technology. Co-founder of AI TriMind.

I have made a lot of decisions in my career. The hard part was never the decision itself. It was deciding without knowing what to expect, and then fixing the consequences. Technology has changed that: today you can walk through the possible scenarios before you commit. That is what we build.

For three and a half years I ran a photo and video studio and event space in Silicon Valley, hosting 15 to 20 events a month. There I wrote the first Business Mindset board game, so that owners would sit at one table and talk about their real businesses. I ran it in person, one on one and in groups, produced it in the United States, and nearly the entire print run sold. The small business owner meetups I have run since then grew a circle of founders in the Valley who keep coming back with their next decision. Several of them changed direction after those sessions and told me so.

Then I went looking for a technical partner and found a team. AI TriMind is now a technology company. The Business Mindset game is digitized, and with it a simulator of decision scenarios, daily practices, and sprints, all inside the TriMind app. The app releases in autumn 2026. Behind it stands a serious engineering team and an agent system of our own.

The Decision Stress Test is the same method, run live, with me.
